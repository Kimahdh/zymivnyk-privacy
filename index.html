Privacy Policy

VOLYA Studio ("we") develops the game Zymivnyk: Winter Camp (the "Game").

  1. Data we collect. None. The Game requires no registration, has no accounts and does not transmit personal data to us or to third parties.
  2. Data stored on your device. Game progress (money, buildings, levels) and settings (sound, vibration, language) are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the Game deletes them.

  4. Ads and purchases. The current version contains no ads and no in-app purchases. If they are added, we will update this policy before that version is released.

  6. Changes. Any new version of this policy is published on this page with a new date.
